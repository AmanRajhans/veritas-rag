# Northstar People Operations Guide

## Expense policy

Employees may purchase ordinary business items up to 100 US dollars without advance approval. Expenses from 100 to 500 dollars require manager approval before purchase. Expenses above 500 dollars require both manager and department-head approval. Airfare, lodging, and conference registration always require advance manager approval regardless of amount.

Itemized receipts are required for expenses of 25 dollars or more. Expense reports should be submitted within thirty calendar days of purchase. Finance may reject reports submitted more than ninety days late unless a vice president approves an exception.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely within their country of employment. Working from another country requires written approval from People Operations and Tax at least thirty days before travel. A temporary international arrangement may last no more than twenty business days in a rolling twelve-month period.

## Learning budget

Each full-time employee receives a 1,500 dollar annual learning budget. Books, technical courses, certification exams, and professional conferences are eligible. Hardware, travel upgrades, and entertainment are not eligible. Unused funds expire on December 31 and do not roll over.

## Paid time off

Planned leave of five or more consecutive business days should be requested at least two weeks in advance. Managers should respond within three business days. Sick leave does not require advance notice, but employees should notify their manager before the start of their local workday when practical.
